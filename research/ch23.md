## 2. The concept as the field holds it

Three habits, each with a field home. All three are **general knowledge, not a study of the record**: the record holds no field study of
super-apps, of platform engineering or of context maps. Its only named field is one line in `STZSUPERAPP_DESIGN.md` (line 13): "FinClip,
WeChat, and kin".

1. **The super-app and the mini-program.** One large application embeds a runtime and runs small applications inside it, sharing its services and
   its store; WeChat's mini programs (launched January 2017) are the model most copy. The design document describes it so before it refuses it
   (`STZSUPERAPP_DESIGN.md`, section 0 and the table of section 1). The host is privileged, the mini-app second-class, the isolation a sandbox.
2. **Bounded contexts and a context map.** Domain-driven design (Eric Evans, *Domain-Driven Design*, 2003) cuts a large system into models, each
   with its own words, and draws a map of how they relate. It is the field's nearest idea to "a world" and "a bond". It lives in diagrams and in
   review, not in a running registry that refuses a call.
3. **The platform team, and the deployment declared as code.** "Platform engineering" names a team that builds the internal base others deploy onto
   (the vocabulary of *Team Topologies*, 2019); infrastructure-as-code tools (Terraform, 2014) let that team declare machines before they exist.
   The person who works across the floors is, in the field, a software architect, a solutions architect or a platform engineer: a title held by a
   person, whose artefacts are diagrams and manifests.

For the scopes, the field's home is the compiler, not the program: Rust's target triples and `cfg(target_os)` (Rust 1.0, 2015; chapter 4 already
uses Rust) and the lint of an Android project that flags a call the minimum platform level lacks (no date given; I did not check it). Each decides
which system the code is *for*. None lets the program name that system at the call site and refuse the act.

The shared shape: the field draws the picture (a host, a map, a diagram) and keeps the judge outside it. In all three the program knows nothing of
its own composition.

### B1 · a world, end to end (claim: a world has a being, a becoming and a way of being met)


### B4 · the crossing: a bond, then three gates (claim: nothing crosses between worlds unless declared and governed, and each refusal names its gate)


## 9. Six doors

  judged (B3). The record supports this door with B2 only.

  are stubs (B9). The persona a world serves is not a field.
- **The decision-maker.** You declare who may cross where and at what risk, in three gates, and every cleared crossing leaves a decision with the actor, the authority and the
  risk of its moment (B4). Your architect's file is text you own (B11).

  (`base\education\SOFTANZA_EDUCATION_PLAN.md`, line 21, the plan quoting an earlier document). Say so, or leave the door to one line: the exercise below is judged by running.
- **The agent.** A world can be wrapped as an agent the host ticks (`05_stzapp_live_narrated`, scenario 4); a deploy by a language-model actor is refused and nothing is
