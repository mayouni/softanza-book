## 2. The concept as the field holds it

Two meanings of "a conversation that asks" meet in the field, and a third practice stands behind the fifth register.

**Slot filling in dialogue frameworks (general knowledge, and the record's one sentence about them).** A dialogue
framework asks the user for the missing pieces of a form until the form is full. The record names the family and
says what it leaves to the designer: "The conversational frameworks of the field (Rasa, Botpress, Dialogflow and kin) own
the dialogue model and ask the designer to write intents and flows in their language." Source:
`drafts/stzsite-roots-and-achievements.md`, 12.25, ruled 2026-10-07 23:17 UTC; the study marks that it described the
field "from the genre", not from the providers' documentation re-read. Treat the three product names as general knowledge;
the chapter may describe the practice and leave the names out.

**Vibe coding (general knowledge).** The human prompts, the model produces, the human keeps what runs. The term was put
into circulation by Andrej Karpathy in February 2025 (general knowledge; the writer should check the date before
printing it). The record gives the inverse a name: `SOFTANZA_INTELLIGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md` §0.3 "Wise Coding (the
deliberate inversion of vibe coding)", header "captured 2026-07-13 from the author's directive". The site page
`D:\GitHub\stzsite\content\en\wise.md` says the same in public words.

**Programming by example (general knowledge, and the record's paragraph).** The field's one famous product is
spreadsheet fill-by-example (the feature is called Flash Fill in Excel; the research line behind it is inductive program
synthesis, Gulwani and others; general knowledge, dates to be checked by the writer). Beside it: programming by
demonstration; the executable example in documentation (doctest) and the test written first; property-based testing as
the complement; and "the model era made the example the way one steers a model, few shots at a time". Source: 12.26, ruled
2026-10-07 23:26 UTC, "Where the field stands" (also from the genre, not re-read).

What the field does not do, in the record's reading: it takes the user's reply as the answer and acts. The record's claim
is that the reply is only a **candidate**, whatever the form it comes in, and the world decides.
