## 2. The concept as the field holds it

The concept: **an organisation judged by its own chart**. A bank's supervisor does not only ask what the bank did; it asks
who answers to whom. A board must exist; internal audit must be independent of management; a risk function must exist; the
people who run operations must not report through the people who hold the money. In the field, that question is answered on
paper (a chart in a slide deck, a governance manual), by a person (an auditor, a compliance officer) or inside a governance,
risk and compliance suite that holds the rule as a questionnaire. The chart and the rule live in different documents, so the
chart can drift, and nobody can say which line broke which rule.

Three practices the field holds (all **general knowledge**, no source in the record; the writer should check the dates before
printing them):

1. **The Basel Committee's corporate governance principles for banks** (Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, July 2015, the
   revision of its 2010 text): a board that oversees, an independent risk function, an independent internal audit function with
   access to the board. It is the text a supervisor's reading of a chart comes from. (general knowledge)
2. **The Three Lines Model** of the Institute of Internal Auditors (July 2020, the update of the earlier "three lines of
   defence"): management and risk functions, then an internal audit that is independent and answers to the governing body. The
   same rule about audit in another vocabulary. (general knowledge)
3. **Policy as code**: the Open Policy Agent project and its policy language (a policy written as rules and evaluated over a
   structured document; graduated at the Cloud Native Computing Foundation in 2021). It is the nearest tool to "a rule as data
   judging a structure", but it judges configuration and requests, not a reporting line. (general knowledge)

The record's own field study of this subject is thin. The narration `stzOrgChartDoc.md` (lines 1008-1030) holds a comparison
table (graph libraries, diagram tools, HR suites) that is **all wins, undated, with uncited cost figures**. Do not use it as
the chapter's field (hazard 8). The honest sentences about the field are in the library's own comments (section 7).

## 7. The field, and where it leads

- **Where Softanza stands.** The chart and its rule meet in one place and the verdict names the rule; the rule is data a bank's
  analyst can write (block 4); the same report serves the bank's chart, code and agents (block 5). A governance suite keeps the

    library claims that a regulator accepted a rule.
  - *Workflow and evidence.* The library judges a chart; it does not route a finding to a person, hold a remediation workflow, or
    keep who-accepted-what. The record has no file for it on this subject. (general knowledge on what suites offer.)
  - *Where it runs.* The bank's product is a Python application (`forged.md:84`), not the library: the library has not yet carried the
    bank's rules into production. "a step ahead, not a step taken" (`forged.md:84`).
- Dates: the library's rule plan P2b names commit `621ee8736`; the Forged page's runs are of 2026-10-04 at stzlib `0e72e2e2c`

  to a regime's rule base, and the court reports it with the rule name and the position (block 4).

  installation guide attests the reference; the library's validators are the lesson it left.
