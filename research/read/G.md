## 32-many-minds-on-one-body-of-work.md

**1. What I remember.** Softanza's last two years were written by many AI sessions on one machine with no framework: files, a history, hooks. Each rule in the protocol cites the dated failure that paid for it (wholesale staging, composed timestamps, judging words, unperceived sound, an eight-minute test loop, a request with no way to check it), and when a rule is broken anyway it becomes a mechanism. The limit: the protocol is proved at one person, one machine; the cost record holds no token count; the author's hand thinned in the code the sessions wrote. I could restate section 3 without looking.

**2. Where I lost the thread.**
- Section 3, "You have already met it if you read the memos at the end of each phase of this book." I had not: no memo appears in the book's pages. A "we have seen" that was not seen.
- Section 3, "the sixth law of the intelligence architecture made runnable." Neither "intelligence architecture" nor its six laws appears on any other page; the phrase is used only here.
- Section 4, "the central session is corrected by the Observer and bound by the doctrine." The Observer, the doctrine, the desks and the estate are all names of the record, none introduced on this page or in the glossary. I cannot say who the Observer is.
- Section 4, "The decisions at the front of this book are marked the same way." Nothing at the front of the book carries such a mark; the preface does not mention delegation. A pointer to a page that does not exist.

**3. Where I doubted.**
- "about fifteen sessions" (section 4) beside "thirteen of the seventeen desks measured" (section 3): seventeen desks measured, fifteen sessions in all.
- "Five weeks after the law, the central session composed a stamp again." The law has no date on the page, so the five weeks hang from nothing I can check.
- The promise-density figures, "nearly a hundred per thousand lines", "seventy-five", "under one": no block shows them; the page says a study measured it and gives no other hold.

**4. Where it moved me.** "a reminder is not a mechanism." And the shape: "A failure, dated. A rule, written where the next session will read it. Then, when the rule is broken anyway, a mechanism." And the loss: "my hand, which was the style, thinned where they wrote." Bored: section 6, "The field, and where it leads", restates section 7 in other words; I would skip it.

**5. The code.** All three blocks are understood from the sentence before them, and each `#-->` is what the sentence promised. `StzCodeIsClean` answering `1` after a finding is the right reveal. Nothing to cut.

**6. Errors I am sure of.**
- The four pointers in item 2 (the memos, the sixth law, the Observer, the decisions at the front). "should be rethinked" is a quotation, marked as such; I leave it.

**7. The door that is mine.** The agent's: "None of it assumes you are careless. All of it assumes you are many." Worth the walk.

**8. Verdict.** `ready after the items above`: four pointers lead nowhere, and the Observer paragraph needs one sentence of introduction.
