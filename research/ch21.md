## 2. The concept as the field holds it

**Promises (the test as an example).** The field's oldest form of "documentation that runs" is the example with its expected output: Python's `doctest`
(a docstring that looks like an interactive session is run and its printed text compared), Rust's documentation tests, Go's testable examples with an
`// Output:` comment (all general knowledge). Their strength is that the example is the specification. Their weakness, which the record states in its own
terms, is that the comparison is text against text and the harness decides what a failure is. The record's own field note is the launch memo of Testoor,
whose plane "learned from the people who test hardest and practises most of what they teach" (`D:\GitHub\softanza\memos\2026-10-05-testoor-launch.md`,
section 1, 2026-10-05).

**Perception (the last gate).** The field's nearest practice is the snapshot or visual-regression test (a screenshot or a rendered tree stored and compared on the
next run; general knowledge): it proves that today's picture equals yesterday's. The record's sentence for this is "A comparison proves SAMENESS. It never proves
RIGHTNESS" (`D:\GitHub\softanza\protocol\PERCEPTION.md`, section 3). The library holds a case: a bar plot drew vertical bars for horizontal data while all 73 of its
examples went on passing (`base/test/_expect.ring`, header), which is why `Shows()` compares the picture exactly. That is sameness made strict. Rightness is a person.

**Security (a claim and its proof).** The field audits against lists. The threat model names its lenses itself: OWASP Top 10:2025, the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic
Applications 2026, the OWASP LLM list, NIST CSF 2.0 (`base/security/SOFTANZA_THREAT_MODEL.md`, table at the top, written 2026-09-29). For detection and evidence the
record has a field study, "The Softanza Incident-Analysis System vs the Field" (`base/doc/design/SOFTANZA_INCIDENT_VS_THE_FIELD.md`, written 2026-08-01), against
SIEMs (Splunk, Elastic), Sigma rules, SOAR and the DFIR toolchain.

**Performance (the instrument).** The field measures from outside: Micrometer, OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, the APM agents, JFR
(`base/doc/design/SOFTANZA_PERF_VS_THE_FIELD.md`, written 2026-07-29, "against the field as of early 2026").
