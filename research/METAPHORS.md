Written 2026-10-11 by grep over `src/en/*.md` in reading order (00, 01..33, A..G), prose only (code fences, inline code and front matter stripped); the French counted the same way over `src/fr`. The author rules on every row: nothing here has been applied to a page.

Reading the columns. **First appearance** is the first prose sentence in reading order (chapter, heading, quote). **Definition** is Appendix D's entry, or the chapter's own sentence when D has none. **Mapped?** says whether that first appearance already maps the metaphor term by term, in the book's words; when it does not, the paragraph to insert there is drafted under "Drafted mapping paragraphs". **French** gives the name from `FRENCH.md` and what `src/fr` does with it. **Refused with it** is the field's image the chapters set aside, by chapter.

## Drafted mapping paragraphs

Each is under seventy words, in the first person, built from Appendix D and the chapter's own sentences; no new fact, image or particular. The author inserts, rewrites or refuses.

**court** (preface, after "person or model."):

> By a court I mean the judge of a thing. It is made from a declaration, and it judges whoever speaks that declaration, a person or a model. It says one of two words, kept or refused, and it says why in the words of the declaration. A guard is a file of assertions that proves a court does this, and section 5 of each chapter lists them.

**door (1), the six doors** (preface, after the list of nine elements):
